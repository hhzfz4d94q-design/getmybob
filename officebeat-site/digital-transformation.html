Healthcare Practice · Service

Digital transformation that sticks.

Operating model, workflow-first sequencing, vendor portfolio rationalization, change management, and KPI design — built from Pfizer-scale digital transformation experience.

Digital transformation has a high failure rate. We know why.

Most healthcare digital-transformation engagements deliver software changes without delivering operational changes. The platforms get rebuilt; the workflows that depend on them don't. The clinicians work around the new system, the operating metrics don't move, and 18 months later the program is quietly rebadged as "the old system was fine."

OfficeBeat's healthcare digital-transformation practice was built inside that exact problem at Pfizer scale. We design transformation programs that integrate workflow, technology, and operating model concurrently — and we hand them off to your team with the playbook to sustain them.

  • Operating model design — the shape of the function that runs the post-transformation reality.
  • Workflow-first sequencing — workflow changes pull the platform, not the other way around.
  • Vendor portfolio rationalization — concentrating the stack, not just adding to it.
  • Change management — clinician and staff adoption as a designed outcome, not a hoped-for one.
  • Metric design — operating KPIs that show whether transformation is real.

What we deliver

Five digital transformation engagements.

OD

Operating model design

The post-transformation org structure, role design, decision rights, and operating cadence. The shape of the muscle.

WS

Workflow-first sequencing

Identify workflow changes that need to happen first; platform changes follow them, not lead them. Reduces failure rate dramatically.

VR

Vendor portfolio rationalization

Strategic concentration of the platform stack, RFP design with risk clauses, contract economics realignment.

CM

Change management

Clinician and staff adoption designed as a deliverable. Training, champion networks, friction-removal cycles, adoption metrics.

KP

Operating KPI design

Pre- and post- transformation KPIs that show whether the program actually moved the operating reality. No vanity metrics.
