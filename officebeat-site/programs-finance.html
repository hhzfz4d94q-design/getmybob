Finance & Banking — Programs Practice

Program Management.

Where risk and transformation programs have to land. Delivery muscle for the moments that actually matter.

Most finance programs don't fail at design. They fail in the middle, when the steering committee stops asking hard questions and the workstreams quietly slip. Our program management practice is built around the middle — the part where independent assurance, credible escalation, and brutal honesty about progress are the difference between a program that lands and one that gets quietly de-scoped.

Large Program Delivery

Large Program Delivery

Regulatory, transformation, and integration programs that have to land — with a credible delivery plan, not a Gantt chart fantasy.

Capabilities

  • Regulatory remediation programs (consent orders, MRAs, MRIAs)
  • Core platform replacements and ledger migrations
  • Treasury, payments, and capital-markets platform rollouts
  • Risk transformation programs (post-merger or post-incident)
  • Multi-vendor SI / consultant orchestration
  • Executive steering, RAID management, and credible escalation paths

Sample deliverables

  • Program charter, target operating model, and stage-gate plan
  • Workstream playbooks with measurable exit criteria per stage
  • Risk and dependency map with named owners and dates
  • Executive steering pack template and reporting cadence
  • Independent program assurance reviews and amber/red recovery plans
