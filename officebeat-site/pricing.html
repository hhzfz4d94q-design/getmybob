Engagement Models

Pricing.

Fixed-fee phases. Rough ranges shared before any proposal. No open-ended retainers.

The fast version.

We work in fixed-fee phases, beginning with a two-week diagnostic. We share rough ranges before we send any proposal. We've never run an open-ended retainer.

Typical engagement economics.

Diagnostic

$15–25K

Two weeks. Senior practitioner. Output: one-page heatmap + costed 90-day plan.

Build phase

$60–150K

Most single-domain rebuilds (ERM, TPRM, cyber, treasury). More for multi-vertical or platform rollouts.

Advisory retainer

Quoted by cadence

Monthly or quarterly. Sized to your team's bandwidth. Reviewed quarterly for value.

What's included by default.

  • Weekly status with named deliverables.
  • All work delivered by senior practitioners — no junior staffing.
  • Steady-state handoff playbook for your team.
  • Travel pass-through at cost (rare; we work remote-first).

What we won't do.

  • Bill by the hour for partner time — phases are fixed-fee.
  • Hide scope creep in change orders.
  • Lock you into year-long contracts to get a reasonable phase price.
  • Take an engagement we know we're not the best fit for.

If your budget doesn't fit.

We'll tell you in the first call. We'll either suggest a scoped-down engagement that delivers real value at the right price, or recommend partners who fit better. The first call is the right place to find this out.

Ready to size up an engagement?

Start with a 60-minute call. We'll share rough numbers up front.
