Our Process

How we work.

The first call, the diagnostic, the build, the handoff — and what we won't do. Removing the anxiety of buying senior consulting for the first time.

The first call.

A senior practitioner takes your inquiry — not an SDR, not a junior, not a chatbot. The call is 60 minutes, no obligation, and is genuinely diagnostic. By the end, you'll know whether we can help, and if we can't, who can.

📅 Book the first call →

If we're a fit.

We send a one-page scope within three business days. It names: the problem we'd take on, the named practitioner who'd lead it, the deliverables, the timeline, and the fixed fee. No bait-and-switch from senior partner pitch to junior staffing. No leveraged pyramids.

The two-week diagnostic.

Every engagement begins with a two-week diagnostic. We interview the people who matter, pull the artifacts that exist, and map the current state against your goals. The deliverable is a one-page heatmap and a costed 90-day plan. From there, you decide whether to keep going. We'd rather earn the next phase than lock you into a year-long contract.

The build (or coach) phase.

We work two ways, depending on what fits your team's bandwidth:

  • Lead the work — fixed-fee phases, weekly status, named deliverables. We do the writing, the design, the implementation oversight, the operational handoff. Your team participates and learns by watching.
  • Coach the work — advisory cadence, your team executes, we review and challenge. Faster knowledge transfer, slower delivery. We help you decide which mode fits.

The handoff.

Steady-state goes to your team. We document a playbook so the program survives staff turnover. We stay on an advisory cadence only if it continues to add value — measured quarterly, by you.

What we won't do.

  • Write you a 200-page framework no one reads.
  • Send junior staff to do partner-led work.
  • Hide scope creep in change orders.
  • Run an engagement that's invisible to your team's day-to-day operating cadence.
  • Take a project we know we're not the best fit for.
Cadence

Week 2Heatmap & planOne-page heatmap, costed 90-day plan, go/no-go conversation with you.
Weeks 3–6FoundationsRisk taxonomy, governance, appetite, KRI design — or the equivalent for your problem.
Weeks 7–10Build / rolloutImplementation in your environment. Weekly status. No surprises.
Weeks 11–13Embed & handoffPlaybook to your team. Board calibration. Steady-state cadence defined.

Engagement economics.

Diagnostics are typically $15–25K depending on scope. Build phases are quoted fixed-fee per phase — most fall in the $60–150K range for a single risk-domain rebuild, more for multi-vertical or platform rollouts. Advisory retainers are quoted by cadence (monthly or quarterly).

We share rough ranges before we send a proposal. If your budget doesn't fit, we'll tell you in the first call and recommend partners who do.
