Third-Party Risk.
Without strangling procurement.

Vendor tiering, due diligence, ongoing monitoring, concentration analytics, and exam prep — TPRM programs that survive scrutiny without grinding the business to a halt.

Why TPRM is the silent budget killer.

Third-party risk used to be a procurement formality — annual questionnaires, a SOC 2 review, a checkbox. That model is dead. Cloud, SaaS, embedded fintech, AI vendors, and concentrated infrastructure providers have made third-party risk the largest unmanaged exposure for most regulated firms. Examiners know it. So do the ratings agencies.

OfficeBeat's TPRM practice builds programs that survive examiner scrutiny without strangling the procurement engine. We've stood up TPRM at the bank-of-bank scale and at the fintech-trying-to-pass-its-first-OCC-exam scale.

  • Vendor tiering — risk-based segmentation that procurement actually uses.
  • Due-diligence workflows — depth proportional to tier, no theater.
  • Ongoing monitoring — continuous, not annual; SOC 2 / cyber / financial signal integration.
  • Concentration analytics — single-point-of-failure mapping across vendors and sub-processors.
  • Renewal & offboarding controls — the part that always falls apart in practice.
  • Regulator-ready evidence — playbooks for OCC, FRB, FDIC, NYDFS, and SR 23-4 alignment.
