EHR strategy is product strategy.

Most healthcare organizations treat the EHR as IT — a system to be maintained, upgraded, and complained about. The organizations that pull ahead treat the EHR as the product that delivers clinical care. The roadmap, the user experience, the integration architecture, and the data layer become competitive assets, not cost centers.

OfficeBeat's EHR & SaaS practice is led by Geetanjali Arora, who spent 23+ years inside this exact problem — including roles as Sr. Director of Digital Solutions at BioReference Laboratories, Director of Product Portfolio at SRS Health / Nextech, and most recently Principal, Digital Transformation at Pfizer. We help health systems, payers, and healthcare SaaS vendors get strategic about platforms most of them are running tactically.

  • EHR product strategy — roadmap design, feature prioritization, vendor / build decisions.
  • SaaS transitions — moving legacy clinical or operational platforms to SaaS, with risk and continuity preserved.
  • D2C and patient-facing layers — the patient experience as a product surface, not a portal afterthought.
  • Clinical-system integrations — HL7, FHIR, claims, labs, imaging — making the data layer actually flow.
  • AI and automation overlays — what to build into the EHR vs. what to leave as adjacent tooling.
