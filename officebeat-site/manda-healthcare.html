Mergers & Acquisitions.

Healthcare M&A diligence and post-deal vendor consolidation, led from inside the work.

Healthcare M&A is unforgiving. The diligence questions that matter — clinical workflows, EHR compatibility, vendor lock-in, regulatory posture — are not the ones generalist M&A advisors ask. Our healthcare M&A practice is led by someone who has shaped product portfolios across BioReference, Nextech, and Pfizer, and who understands what a clinical-system decommissioning timeline actually looks like.

Diligence

Healthcare M&A Diligence

Operational and clinical-tech diligence — the questions that get missed when the deal team isn't from healthcare.

Capabilities

  • Operational diligence on clinical, RCM, and tech operations
  • EHR and clinical-system landscape diligence
  • Vendor concentration and integration cost diligence
  • HIPAA, HITECH, 42 CFR Part 2 compliance state
