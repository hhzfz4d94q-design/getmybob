Why treasury is the next modernization battleground.

Treasury sits at the intersection of three accelerating forces: real-time payments (RTP, FedNow, ISO 20022), API-first banking (bank connectivity, embedded finance, ERP integrations), and tightening operational-risk expectations (FRB SR 11-7-style model governance now bleeding into payments). Most corporate and bank treasury operations were architected for a slower, batch-based world. They no longer fit.

OfficeBeat's Treasury practice helps banks, fintechs, and corporate treasuries modernize this layer without re-introducing the operational, fraud, or settlement risk that early movers learned the hard way. We've sat in the seat: capital markets settlement, commercial bank treasury services, ERP-bank integration, and TMS rollouts.

  • Cash management modernization — visibility, forecasting, sweep automation.
  • Payments rails diligence — ACH, wires, RTP, FedNow, SWIFT, ISO 20022.
  • Liquidity & intraday risk — counterparty exposure, settlement risk, LCR/NSFR alignment.
  • FX risk frameworks — exposure measurement, hedge program design, accounting hedge testing.
  • Treasury operations risk & controls — segregation of duties, dual-control, fraud playbooks.
  • TMS & bank connectivity — Kyriba, GTreasury, FIS Quantum, Trovata; SWIFT, host-to-host, APIs.

How we engage

A two-week diagnostic. Then a clear path.

Every treasury engagement begins with a focused 2-week diagnostic. We map your current state — payment volumes, bank-account topology, TMS posture, control environment, exception rates, and forecast accuracy — against your goals. The deliverable is a one-page heatmap and a prioritized 90-day plan.
