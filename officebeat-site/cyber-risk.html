Cyber risk.
Decision-grade, not tool-grade.

NIST CSF 2.0 maturity, Zero Trust strategy, incident response playbooks that survive contact, and board-level reporting that actually gets read.

Cyber risk is a board-level problem dressed as a technical one.

The vendors will sell you tools. The CISO conferences will sell you frameworks. What's missing in most organizations is the bridge: a risk-governance layer that translates technical posture into language the board, the examiner, the auditor, and the customer can all act on.

OfficeBeat's cyber risk practice operates at that bridge. We're framework-fluent (NIST CSF 2.0, ISO 27001, CIS, FFIEC CAT) but we don't sell frameworks. We build programs that maintain the security-first posture you need while staying defensible to the examiner you'll face next quarter.

  • NIST CSF 2.0 maturity — diagnostic, target-state design, prioritized remediation.
  • FFIEC CAT alignment — for U.S. financial institutions.
  • Zero Trust architecture — strategy, segmentation, IAM rationalization.
  • Incident response playbooks — tested with tabletop, not just written.
  • Cyber insurance readiness — pre-application diagnostics, premium reduction posture.
  • Board-level cyber reporting — quarterly artifacts the board actually reads.

Eight cyber engagements.

Architecture design, segmentation planning, IAM rationalization, phased rollout, success-metric definition.
