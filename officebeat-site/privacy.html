Privacy Policy.

What we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, your rights.

Last updated: May 22, 2026

What we collect

When you fill out a form on this site (contact, assessment, subscription), we collect the information you provide — typically name, email, company, role, and message — plus standard browser metadata (IP address, user agent, referring page) and a HubSpot tracking cookie that helps us attribute visits to inquiries.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry.
  • To send follow-up communications if you've requested them (e.g., insights subscription).
  • To improve the site and understand what content is useful.

Who we share it with

OfficeBeat does not sell or rent personal data. We use the following service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • HubSpot — CRM, form processing, email delivery. HubSpot privacy policy.
  • Microsoft 365 — email hosting (team@officebeatllc.com). Microsoft privacy policy.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you by emailing team@officebeatllc.com. If you're an EU/UK resident, your rights under GDPR / UK GDPR apply.

Cookies

This site uses a HubSpot cookie (hubspotutk) to attribute visits to form submissions. We do not use advertising or retargeting cookies.

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change. The "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about privacy: team@officebeatllc.com.

Questions?
