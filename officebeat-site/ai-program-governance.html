AI program governance.
The missing layer.

Use-case intake, model validation, continuous monitoring, vendor AI risk, shadow AI mitigation, and board reporting — the operating cadence between AI strategy and model risk.

AI governance is the missing layer.

Most organizations have an AI strategy. Most have a model-risk policy. What's usually missing is the program-governance layer in the middle: the operating cadence that decides which AI use cases get green-lit, who owns them, how performance and risk are monitored, and how the board hears about it.

OfficeBeat's AI governance practice draws on three converging streams: model risk experience from banking (SR 11-7 lineage), digital-product governance from healthcare SaaS, and operational risk discipline from broader ERM work. We design AI program governance that fits the regulatory landscape you're actually in, not the one a generic framework imagines you're in.

  • AI use-case intake & triage — risk-based gating that procurement and product teams will use.
  • Model risk lifecycle — validation, monitoring, retirement; scaled to the model's risk tier.
  • AI-specific operational risk — vendor concentration, prompt injection, data leakage, hallucination cost models.
  • Board-level AI reporting — quarterly cadence, executable thresholds, no fear-mongering.
  • Cross-vertical applicability — finance, healthcare, and SaaS-platform AI governance.
